2026 R&B Breakout Artists

Mariah the Scientist

Mariah Amani Buckles known professionally as Mariah the Scientist, is an American singer and songwriter. Her 2025 singles, "Burning Blue" and "Is It a Crime" (with Kali Uchis), marked her first entries on the Billboard Hot 100 as a lead artist; they both peaked within its top 30. Both songs preceded her fourth studio album, Hearts Sold Separately (2025). Below is her website where she sells merch, notifies fans of tour dates and so much more.

KWN

Khyra Leah Wilson, known professionally as kwn (pronounced kay-wuhn) and formerly k1, is a British singer, songwriter, rapper, and producer. She was nominated for Best New International Act at the 2025 BET Awards, was named Billboard's R&B Rookie of the Month, and was included on the NME 100 list of "essential emerging artists".
