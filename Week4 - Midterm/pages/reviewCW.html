REVIEW

Mikha'la Campbell 04/21/2026

Catwoman (2004), the first film where Black woman solo headlines in Catwoman film, is one of my recent watches and what I'd consider to be one of my comfort movies. Although popularly known as a "commercial failure" because of the poor CGI and major plot holes, I believe that's what makes the film comfort movie and not necessarily a serious watch.

Even though it was a box office flop, I genuinely believe that is a movie that is so bad that it's so good. You have to adondon the DC orgin and take it as a standalone film separate from the franchise to truly enjoy it. Just take it for what it is. Although, I must say I did not love the ending with her breaking up with the detective, but I guess it makes sense with her choosing a life of being free and untamed.

Trivia

  • A "Different" Catwoman: This film does not feature Selina kyle, but instead a new character named Patience Phillips.
  • Golden Rasberry Award: Halle Berry accepted the Golden Rasberry Award for Worst Actress in 2005, famously bringing her Oscar along as she accepted the award.
  • No Batman: They purposefully excluded Batman or any other Gotham characters to create a completely fresh, standalone story.
  • Stunts: Halle Berry had a male stunt double.
