REVIEW

Mikha'la Campbell 04/21/2026

B.A.P.S is a comedic, heartwarming, naturally hilarious movie, and it is most definitely one of my favorite movies. I have watched it countless times and I have laughed the same every single time. It seems a little corny and predictable in the beginning, but it has a unique twist towards the end. This movie gives a certain nostaglia and peaks my interest especially as it showcases these beautiful black woman being so proud of their cultural identity and expression.

This movie was rated as a terrible movie in its time, but its competitiors were Titanic, Air Force One, and Men in Black, which are incredible and timeless movies. Therefore, I don't think it was given a fair chance but I believe it is a must watch for anyone who has not watched it before. (Only if you're lighthearted and don't take film-watching serious at least.)

Trivia

  • B.A.P.S: The title stands for "Black American Princesses", ironically Natalie Desselle played the Step-sister of Cinderella that same year.
  • Cameos: The movie features appearances from Bernie Mac, LL Cool J, Dennis Rodeman, Heavy D etc.
  • Comdey: Halle Berry's first leading role in a comedy.
  • Classic: Black film lovers made the movie a cult classic despite the awful ratings the movie first received.
